How it works

Three simple steps, zero heavy lifting on your end.

  1. I collect

    I come to your home and personally collect your boxes, albums, and bags of photos.

  2. I scan

    Your photos are professionally scanned one by one, handled with the utmost care.

  3. I return

    Your originals come back safely, plus a digital copy you can keep, share, and pass down.
